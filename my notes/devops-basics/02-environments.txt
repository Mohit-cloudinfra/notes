========================================================================
  DEVOPS BASICS  >  ENVIRONMENTS
========================================================================

  IN ONE LINE
  The same application is deployed to a chain of environments, each
  with a wider audience, before the public ever sees it.


  1. THE CHAIN
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

      DEV  >  QA  >  SIT  >  UAT  >  PERF  >  PRE-PROD  >  PROD


  2. SIR'S DRINK STORY
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

      You invent a new drink.

      You taste the formula yourself                     DEV
      Employees taste it, you take feedback              QA
      Friends and family taste it, you take feedback     SIT
      A random group tastes it, you take feedback        UAT
      FSSAI certification                                client approval
      Release to the public market                       PROD


  [+] INTERVIEW
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  [+] Q1. What environments does an application go through?

          DEV, QA, SIT, UAT, PERF, PRE-PROD, PROD. The same build
          moves through each one, tested by a wider group each time,
          and reaches PROD only after the client approves in UAT.


  [+] Q2. What is the difference between SIT and UAT?

          SIT   System Integration Testing. The testing team checks
                that all the components work TOGETHER.
          UAT   User Acceptance Testing. The business or the client
                checks that it does what THEY asked for.

          SIT asks "does it work?". UAT asks "is it what we wanted?".


  [+] Q3. Why have a PRE-PROD when you already tested everywhere?

          PRE-PROD is kept as close to PROD as possible: same
          configuration, similar data and size. It catches the
          problems that only appear in production-like conditions,
          before real users see them.


  [+] Q4. Which environments have you worked with?

          BE HONEST: my lab has a single environment. Say that, then
          explain the chain above and why each step exists.


  REMEMBER
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
      Each step = more people, more feedback, less risk at PROD.
